Nested chess · 2×2 benchmark

4×2 and 4×4 benchmark

Global negamax versus local-only Fairy-Stockfish, with matching local-only controls. Four columns in both layouts: eight boards on 4×2, sixteen on 4×4.

Method

Each condition has 100 games, split evenly by color. A win earns one point, a draw half a point. Win rate and match score have conservative 95% bounds based on color-swapped pairs.

The current bots use the same presets as the 2×2 study. Global search keeps all legal moves, with a 100,000-node cap. Games end under the actual rules, without evaluation adjudication or a move-limit draw.

Intervals describe these runs under shared machine load. They are per condition, assume independent color pairs, and do not establish optimal play or calibrated Elo.
