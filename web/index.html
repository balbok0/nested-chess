Rules

  1. Players alternate globally. White, Black, White, Black—even when one board gets several moves by the same color.
  2. The landing square selects the next board. On a 2 × 2 grid, a quadrant selects a board. On 4 × 4, each 2 × 2 region does. On 8 × 8, every square has its own board. Coordinates use White’s orientation.
  3. Capture a king to win a board. There is no check or checkmate. Kings can stand next to each other. Castling ignores attacks.
  4. An unavailable destination gives a free choice. If the destination is finished or has no moves for you, choose any board where you can move.
  5. Scoring. A win earns 1 point; a draw earns ½ point for each player. The match ends when one score exceeds half the total boards, or all boards finish. Equal final scores draw the match.
  6. Agreed draws. In local two-player mode, offer a draw on the viewed board and let the other player accept or decline. Acceptance closes that board without using a turn.

En passant is available only on the next global turn, on that same board. A board draws after 100 local moves without a pawn move or capture. Threefold repetition of the entire nested position draws unfinished boards; so does having no move anywhere.

En passant example: after Black plays d7–d5, a White pawn on e5 may capture on d6, removing the pawn on d5. Routing must allow White to play on that board immediately; otherwise the opportunity expires.

Fairy-Stockfish analyzes one local board at a time and assumes alternating local turns. It does not account for routing or future repeat turns. Engine source · GPL license
