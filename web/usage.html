Foreground clients are visible browser tabs or foreground apps seen in the last 90 seconds. Signed-in accounts are deduplicated across clients; guests have no account count. Clients are not a count of individual people.

An online match counts once when either player is active. A local game counts after its first move and until it finishes. Each local client runs one game, including when two people share a device. Clients that disable usage sharing or cannot reach the server are absent.

Refreshes every 30 seconds. Activity records are temporary; no moves or positions are collected.
