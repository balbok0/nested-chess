Method

Four conditions: two layouts × two local opponents. The fallback has no difficulty setting. Each condition has 100 scored games, 50 per color. Match score counts a win as 1 and a draw as ½; win rate counts only wins. The tables include the unchanged earlier global and local controls. Confidence bounds are conservative 95% bounds on color-paired outcomes, per condition. They assume independent pairs and do not cover the whole matrix simultaneously.

Forty unscored pilots check determinism: five games per color in each condition. 0 of four conditions required forced two-ply classical openings. All games end under the actual rules; there is no move-limit draw or evaluation adjudication.

Release build, 24 parallel games, single-threaded Fairy-Stockfish 1.1.12, NNUE off. Strong local: skill 10 / 300 ms. Maximum local: skill 20 / 900 ms. Free board choice uses legal_boards[global_ply % legal_boards.len()]. The fallback chooses across all currently legal boards. Rules, routing, and the opponent policy are unchanged.

The earlier controls ran separately. Host load and stochastic engine play can affect time-limited search. These observations do not isolate a causal effect of negamax, prove equivalence, or establish calibrated Elo. Fallback only is a distinct whole-game policy; the earlier global bot could still complete search on some turns.
