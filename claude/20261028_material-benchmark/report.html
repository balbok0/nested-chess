Method

Six conditions, 100 scored games each, 50 per color. Win rate counts wins only; match score counts a win as 1 and a draw as ½. Conservative 95% bounds use the same color-paired Bonferroni–Clopper–Pearson method as earlier studies, per condition, assuming independent pairs. They do not cover the whole matrix simultaneously.

Search, alpha-beta, move ordering, capture-priority fallback, heuristic odds mapping and dynamic match-score aggregation are unchanged. No positional terms or quiescence are added. Casual: depth 2 / 600 ms; Strong: depth 4 / 1,600 ms; Maximum: depth 6 / 4,000 ms. All have a 100,000-node cap and keep only the last completed iteration.

Local Strong uses skill 10 / 300 ms; local Maximum uses skill 20 / 900 ms. Free-board selection is legal_boards[global_ply % legal_boards.len()]. Release build, 24 parallel games; Fairy-Stockfish 1.1.12, one thread, NNUE off.

Sixty unscored determinism pilots: five games per color per condition. 0 of six conditions required forced two-ply classical openings. Games finish under actual rules, without evaluation adjudication or a move-limit draw.

The earlier controls ran separately. Time budgets, host load, stochastic opponent play, and removal of Fairy-Stockfish’s suggested-move ordering can affect results. Point differences do not isolate a causal effect of replacing leaf scores. The heuristic material-to-odds conversion is uncalibrated; these results establish neither optimal play nor Elo.
